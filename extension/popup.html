Save your recovery key
This key encrypts your TOTP secrets before they leave your device. The server never sees them unencrypted. Save it somewhere safe (password manager, notes app). You'll need it to sync on a new device, and it cannot be recovered if lost.
⚠ Store this key before continuing. Without it, your synced data will be unreadable on other devices.
